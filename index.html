REFERENCE COMPARATOR

Granulocyte morphology

Use the reference image and key features during practical training.

Neutrophil

  • Usually 2–5 nuclear lobes
  • Fine, pale granules
  • Segmented nucleus is a key clue

Eosinophil

  • Usually bilobed nucleus
  • Large orange-red granules
  • Coarse granules are conspicuous

Basophil

  • Coarse blue-purple granules
  • Granules may obscure nucleus
  • Dense basophilic granule pattern
LEARNING MODE

Granulocyte quick guide

🔵 Neutrophil

Nucleus: segmented, commonly 2–5 lobes.

Granules: fine and pale.

Teaching clue: nuclear segmentation.

🟠 Eosinophil

Nucleus: usually bilobed.

Granules: large, coarse, orange-red.

Teaching clue: prominent eosinophilic granules.

🟣 Basophil

Nucleus: often difficult to see.

Granules: coarse dark blue-purple.

Teaching clue: dense basophilic granules.
